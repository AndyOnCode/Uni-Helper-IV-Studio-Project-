Problem

```
Many students use different apps to stay organized. They
might use one app to schedule tasks and exams, another to
track their expenses, and a different one to get campus
info. Some even just trust their memory for all of this,
which we really don't recommend. That is why we created
this program: to put everything in one single place. Our
goal is to help students manage their college life without
the need to jump between multiple apps and feeling even
more disorganized.
```

# `Overview` 

```
In the domain of university education, college students
need to manage different academic and personal
```

```
responsibilities, such as completing assignments, managing
their schedules, and controlling their expenses. Because
students often have several responsibilities at the same
time, they need tools that help them manage their time,
organize their tasks, prioritize activities, and keep track
of their academic progress.
```

# `Background` 

```
Managing college life requires coordinating three primary
dimensions of a student's daily routine: temporal
assignments(schedules), financial expenditures (budgets),
and spatial orientation (campus locations). In existing
workflows, unaccounted daily spending. To build a unified
system, we represent each domain using discrete data
structures, standardized units, and explicit balance
equations.
```

```
Analyst Comparison
```

```
To evaluate the student's performance, the system records
tasks and study activities in a history file. The total
duration of the tracking period is called the Simulation
Days. One measure of overall performance is the total
number of completed tasks (Total Completed Tasks). Another
measure is the TCT divided by the simulation days, called
Productivity per Day, and performance per individual
subject can also be measured by dividing the completed
tasks of a specific course by the days invested in it.
```
